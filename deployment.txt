=======================================================
L.M. TAILOR BILLING SYSTEM - DEPLOYMENT & UPDATE GUIDE
=======================================================

This guide explains how to properly update your live application on Vercel and ensure your security settings are active.

-------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: ADDING/UPDATING ENVIRONMENT VARIABLES IN VERCEL
-------------------------------------------------------
You only need to do this step once per new variable, or if you want to change your password in the future!

1. Log into your Vercel Account.
2. Go to the 'lm-tailor-billing-system' Project Dashboard.
3. Click on the "Settings" tab at the top.
4. On the left sidebar, click "Environment Variables".
5. Add your Admin Password:
   - Key: ADMIN_PASSWORD
   - Value: [Type your desired secure password here]
   - Click the black "Add Environment Variable" or "Save" button.

6. Add your Secure Session Key (Mandatory for high security):
   - Key: JWT_SECRET
   - Value: [Type a long random string, e.g., MySuperSecretLMTailorKey2026!]
   - Click the "Add Environment Variable" or "Save" button.

-------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: PUSHING YOUR CODE UPDATES
-------------------------------------------------------
1. Push your latest code changes from your computer to your connected GitHub/GitLab repository.
2. Vercel is usually set to automatically build and deploy new code when you push to the 'main' branch on GitHub.

-------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: REDEPLOYING (If adding new Environment Variables)
-------------------------------------------------------
If you just pushed new code, Vercel will automatically redeploy. HOWEVER, if you ONLY added/changed Environment Variables (like changing your ADMIN_PASSWORD) and didn't change any code, you must manually restart Vercel for it to pick up the new password:

1. In Vercel, go to the "Deployments" tab.
2. Find the topmost deployment (the most recent one).
3. Click the 3 dots (...) on the right side of that row.
4. Click "Redeploy".
5. Uncheck "Use Build Cache" (if the option pops up) and confirm.

-------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4: VERIFICATION
-------------------------------------------------------
1. Once the deployment says "Ready", open your live website link.
2. Because of the new security updates, you will automatically be asked to log in.
3. Type in the newly set ADMIN_PASSWORD. 
4. Your data (Customers, Orders, Alterations) will all be there, perfectly safe!

=======================================================
Note: Your database data (Turso/LibSQL) is completely separate from Vercel deployments. Deploying updates will NEVER erase your database!
